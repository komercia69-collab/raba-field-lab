# RABA Field Lab

**Status:** public research interface / bounded external research environment / non-canonical

RABA Field Lab is the external research and reality-contact layer of the Responsibility-Aware Business Architecture (RABA) research project.

Its purpose is to make bounded cases, comparative studies, and observable research deltas inspectable without treating external activity as authority over RABA.

## What belongs here

- bounded real or representative cases that are explicitly permitted for publication;
- frozen baselines recorded before analysis;
- structured before/after research records;
- comparative studies and external challenge material suitable for a public research surface;
- research outputs that may inform later RABA review.

## What does not follow from presence here

Material in this repository is not automatically:

- RABA canon;
- validation of RABA;
- adoption of RABA;
- certification or compliance evidence;
- a partnership, endorsement, or commercial commitment;
- permission to modify the RABA core architecture.

Field Lab results may generate a **research delta candidate** for controlled review. They do not directly change RABA.

> **Field Lab can challenge RABA. Field Lab cannot modify RABA.**

## Research path

`External reality → Field Lab → observable research delta → controlled RABA intake`

A case should preserve what was known before the interaction, what happened during the bounded interaction, what changed afterward, what did not change, and what remains uncertain.

An `After` state is not assumed to be an improvement. Legitimate outcomes include no meaningful difference, hypothesis falsified, an external approach performing better, or insufficient evidence.

## Repository structure

- [`cases/`](cases/) — public case records that have separate publication permission.
- [`research/`](research/) — comparative and cross-case research outputs.
- [`templates/`](templates/) — minimal intake, baseline, and before/after record templates.
- [`GOVERNANCE.md`](GOVERNANCE.md) — the boundary between the Field Lab and RABA core.

No real case is included in the initial v0.1 structure.
