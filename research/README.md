# Research

**Status:** comparative research area / non-canonical

This directory is for cross-case analysis, comparative studies, and public research outputs derived from bounded Field Lab work.

Typical contents may include:

- framework-to-framework comparisons;
- recurring failure-pattern analysis;
- cross-case deltas;
- external challenge summaries;
- evidence maps that support public inspection.

Research material here must distinguish documented evidence from interpretation and must not treat external agreement, repository activity, or repeated observations as RABA canon.

A research output may become a **research delta candidate** for later controlled RABA review. That status does not itself authorize any change to the RABA core.
