# RABA Field Lab Governance

**Status:** operational boundary for Field Lab v0.1 / non-canonical

## Purpose

The Field Lab is a public external research environment for bounded cases, comparative studies, and observable research deltas around RABA.

It is deliberately separated from the RABA core repository.

## Core boundary

> **Field Lab can challenge RABA. Field Lab cannot modify RABA.**

No external contribution, case result, review, benchmark, agreement, repository activity, or public reaction automatically changes RABA status, architecture, or canon.

## Permitted influence path

`external input → frozen evidence → bounded study → observed delta → research delta candidate → controlled internal review → Human Owner decision`

Only after separate internal review and explicit Human Owner authorization may a Field Lab result influence the RABA core.

## Publication boundary

A real or representative case may appear publicly only when publication permission is explicit and the published form respects any agreed anonymization, confidentiality, and scope limits.

Permission to discuss a case privately is not permission to publish it.

Permission to publish one artifact is not permission to publish related correspondence, identities, attachments, or downstream materials.

## Authority boundary

Field Lab activity does not constitute:

- validation or certification of RABA;
- adoption of RABA;
- endorsement by a participant;
- partnership or commercial commitment;
- compliance evidence;
- canonicalization;
- authorization to change the RABA core.

External evidence may challenge or invalidate an internal assumption. External evidence does not itself authorize the replacement architecture.

## Research integrity

Where practical, case material should preserve:

1. the original intake or neutral description;
2. a frozen baseline before framework-specific analysis;
3. the bounded interaction or comparison;
4. an observable before/after record;
5. what did not change;
6. remaining uncertainty.

Negative and null results are valid Field Lab outcomes.

## Human Owner authority

AI may assist with analysis, structuring, comparison, drafting, and evidence mapping. Final decisions about RABA architecture, publication scope, status promotion, and canonicalization remain with the Human Owner.
